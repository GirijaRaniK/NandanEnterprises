Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how YOUR BRAND collects, uses and protects information when you visit or use our website and services.

At YOUR BRAND, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy describes the general manner in which information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website, contact us or make an enquiry about our products and services.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Information provided in an enquiry or quotation request.
  • Product requirements, customization details and bulk-order information.
  • Basic technical information about your visit to our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided by you may be used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to product and service enquiries.
  • Providing quotations and product information.
  • Understanding customization requirements.
  • Communicating with you about your enquiry or order.
  • Improving our website, products and services.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may provide WhatsApp links so that you can contact us directly regarding products, customization and bulk orders.

When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is handled through WhatsApp and is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

4. Email Communication

If you contact us by email, we may use the information contained in your email to respond to your enquiry, provide quotations or assist with your product requirements.

5. Cookies and Website Technology

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage and improve the user experience.

You can control or disable cookies through the settings available in your web browser. Some website features may not function as intended if certain cookies are disabled.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, process an enquiry, comply with applicable legal requirements or protect our legitimate business interests.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party services, including WhatsApp or other external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, some information may be necessary for us to respond to an enquiry or provide a requested service.

If you would like us to review or update information that you have provided to us, you can contact us using the details provided below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact YOUR BRAND.
